💑 Our Story

A journey filled with love, laughter, and memories.

✨ The First Meeting

Our story began when my father spontaneously introduced us and set me up to accompany you on a trip with your friends. At first, I was concerned because I didn’t want to be an inconvenience. Friend groups usually prefer spending time together for a more intimate bonding experience, and I was a complete stranger. But despite that, you still agreed to let me come along. That was the first time I thought you were genuinely friendly.

💕 The First Week

During our first week of knowing each other, we were still figuring things out. There were moments of awkwardness, but as the days passed, we started getting more comfortable. We slowly became closer, sharing little jokes and conversations. This was the week I realized that we would get along really well.

🌸 The Second Week

By this time, we were already comfortable with each other. You had even started teasing me a lot. Strangely enough, I actually enjoyed it. I couldn’t explain why, but every time you teased me, I just found myself laughing. Maybe it was because it was you. A lot had happened in just two short weeks, but one thing was certain. I was drawn to you more and more.
